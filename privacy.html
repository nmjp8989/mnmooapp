Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

mnmoo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and all users of our educational mobile application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from children or any users. The App operates entirely offline and does not transmit any user data to our servers.

The App may use the following locally on your device:

  • Subscription status — Stored locally to determine access to premium content.
  • Game progress and scores — Stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service, which may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat — Used for in-app subscription management. Their privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is designed for children in grades K–8. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. All purchase and subscription features are protected by a parental gate requiring an adult to verify their age before proceeding.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited data stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers except through the third-party services listed above.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
